About

Picture Perfect Photography

An independent photography practice based north of Dallas, working across Collin and Denton counties and the wider metroplex.

The approach is simple. Turn up prepared, understand what matters about the day before it starts, shoot it properly on professional full-frame equipment, and hand back a gallery that is edited, consistent and ready to use.

Every session and event is covered with dual card backup and redundant storage, because some moments do not come round twice.
